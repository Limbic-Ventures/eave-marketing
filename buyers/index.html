Eave for Buyers

Buying a home? Know it before you own it.

The listing tells you the square footage and the kitchen. It doesn’t tell you the flood zone, the tax rate, what the disclosures actually say, or what the roof will cost. Eave does, for every house you’re considering, side by side. When you close, the one you chose arrives in Eave already known.

Start your search →I’m a buyer’s agent

The case

Why this, and not the portal

Offer on what you know, not on the listing.

You put in an offer when you are confident in what you are buying: everything that is right about the house, and everything that will need work. A listing is written to sell, and what it leaves out about the house and the area, or has quietly stopped showing, is exactly what changes your number. Eave gives you the whole picture, sourced and dated, so the decision is yours and fully informed.

Nobody prices the fix before the offer.

Every repair estimator waits for a finished inspection report, which only exists after your offer is accepted. Eave reads the photos, the disclosures, and whatever your agent has in hand, and gives you a rough range for what each home would cost to bring up before you decide which one to offer on.

Your home arrives already known.

Everything Eave learned about the house you chose comes with you into ownership: the documents, the facts, the walk-through, the roof age three people disagreed about. Nothing to re-enter. Nothing to start over.

From the first showing to the keys

How it works

01 · THE HUNT

Every house you’re considering, in one place

Add a home by address, by the listing link, or by the PDF your agent emailed. Shortlist it, tour it, or pass, with the reason kept so you never re-tour a house you already ruled out.

02 · THE GROUND AND THE COUNTY

What the listing never says

Flood zone, fire severity, seismic, weather and air, school district, county tax-rate estimate, electric territory, broadband. Every value shows where it came from and when. Estimates say estimate. A blank shows a dash, never a zero.

03 · THE DOCUMENTS

Drop the seller’s packet on the house

Pre-inspection, termite, disclosures, HOA, title. Eave sorts 46 kinds of document, reads them when you ask, and puts the facts on the candidate instead of in your inbox. When two documents disagree about the roof, you get a decision to make, not a silent overwrite.

04 · THE COST

What it’ll really cost, as a range

Walk-through photos and your agent’s notes become a list of what needs attention, each with a cost band from a regional table and the evidence behind it. Compare the top three on list price plus what it takes to bring the house up, and export the comparison as a PDF.

05 · THE ESCROW

The folder that finally has version control

Under contract, the same workspace becomes the escrow folder: a checklist by stage, contingency dates as reminders, a daily digest, and every revised document replacing the old one with what changed shown.

06 · THE CLOSE

Your home arrives already known

At close, the winning house becomes your Eave home with its systems, documents, findings, and providers already in place. The other candidates archive with the search. If the deal falls through, nothing is lost. You are back on your shortlist with everything kept.

Rough ranges from photos and documents. Not an inspection. Not an appraisal. Cost ranges come from photos, documents, and a curated regional table. Get an inspection and an appraisal before you buy.

Questions

Ask your agent, in context

Every question you would text your agent lives on the house it is about, with an answer and a status. Your agent’s recommendations land the same way. No chat, no scroll-back, and nothing lost when the thread gets long.

Who runs it

With your agent, or on your own

With your agent

If your agent runs their buyers in Eave, they will invite you and your search is waiting when you sign in. They can line up homes and drop in the seller’s packet before you have an account. The search is yours at close.

On your own

The things buyers actually ask

Questions, answered.

I haven’t picked a house yet. Is this for me?

Yes. That is exactly when it helps. Line up the homes you are considering and Eave answers the same questions about each of them: what the ground and the county say, what the documents say, and what it will roughly cost to bring the house up.

Do I need my agent to use Eave?

No. If your agent runs their buyers in Eave, they will invite you and your search is waiting when you sign in. If they don’t, start one yourself.

Where do the flood, fire, and tax numbers come from?

Official zone maps and county records, each labelled with its source and date. Eave shows the official designation and links the model behind it. It never presents a vendor’s risk score as a fact, and an estimate always says estimate.

Is the cost estimate an inspection?

No. It is a range from photos and documents, with the evidence shown beside it. Rough ranges from photos and documents. Not an inspection. Not an appraisal. Get a licensed inspection before you rely on a number to make an offer.

What happens to the seller’s documents?

They stay on the house they are about. Eave reads a document only when you ask, keeps every original, and shows what changed when a newer version arrives. Your notes are private unless you share one, and the search is yours at close. How documents are handled →
