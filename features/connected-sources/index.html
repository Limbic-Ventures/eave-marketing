Sources · email & receipts

The scattered-record problem

It’s all somewhere.
Just not together.

Receipts sit in email. Manuals sit in a drawer. The contractor who fixed the sewer line is a business card on the fridge. The flood zone is on a federal map you’ve never looked at. None of it is lost, exactly. It just isn’t in one place when the furnace stops on a Sunday.

Sources treats all of it the same way. A linked account, a photo you take, and a public record are each one more source for the same home record, and each one shows where it came from.

One connection does most of the work. Start with Gmail, the inspection report, or a single receipt.

How it works

Bring it in, read it,
check it, file it.

01

Start with one

A new home sees three ways in: connect Gmail, upload your inspection report, or scan a receipt. Any one of them fills in a lot.
02

Eave reads it

A receipt gives the store, the date, the total, and each line item with its model number and price, sorted into appliance, tool, material, or consumable.
03

You check it

Finds wait in a review queue. Save what’s right, skip what isn’t. For an inspection, uncheck anything the seller already fixed.
04

It lands in the record

A saved receipt adds items to the home’s registry, matched to what you already have by model number, then by name, so the same dishwasher isn’t added twice.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

What can I connect right now?

Gmail, Google Calendar, SmartThings, your Amazon order history, and pros from Thumbtack, Angi, Yelp, and Houzz. Home Depot and Lowe’s orders come in through the Gmail scan, and any calendar app can take a downloaded .ics file. Outlook, Google Home and Nest, and Alexa are coming soon. More on sources →

What if I don’t want to connect anything?

Then don’t. Everything in Eave can be built from what you hand over: receipt photos, documents, a room photo, and notes you type or say. Linking an account saves typing; it isn’t required.

What does Eave read from my email?

With read-only Google sign-in, Eave scans the last year of your inbox for receipts, orders, and invoices, and shows you each one to save or skip. It never sends email for you and never changes anything in your inbox. Connecting Gmail for receipts →

Does anything save without my review?

Most sources wait for you: Gmail finds, receipt photos, pros, SmartThings devices, and inspection findings. An Amazon order-history file saves straight to purchase history, and a room scan fills in the room. You can edit or remove any of it afterward.

Can I upload my home inspection report?

Yes. Eave reads the systems with their install years and condition, plus every finding with its severity. Uncheck anything the seller fixed, and nothing saves without your say. See importing a home inspection report and turning it into an upkeep plan.

Does my calendar sync both ways?

No. Eave sends your upkeep tasks to Google Calendar as recurring all-day events. For Apple Calendar or another app, download an .ics file of the schedule.

Do I need to pay to use Sources?

No. Every source works on the Free plan, with a daily limit on how much Eave reads. Eave Intelligence, at $2.99 a month or $24 a year, adds the upkeep plan, the ranked queue, and reading voice and photos on the Capture screen. See pricing →
