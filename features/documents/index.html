Documents · a file

The paper problem

The answers are already written down.
Nobody has time to read them.

A house comes with a stack of paper: the closing packet, the inspection, the disclosures, the manuals, the warranties, the permits. Almost every answer you’ll need later is in there somewhere, usually on page nine.

Document intelligence reads the stack for you, and keeps the trail back to the original so you can check any fact it filed.

How it works

Recognize it, read it,
file it, keep it.

01

Drop it in

A PDF, a scan, or a phone photo of the page. Upload the same file twice and Eave uses the copy already on file.
02

Eave works out what it is

It looks at the first pages and decides the kind, who sent it, and its date. Not sure? It offers its best guesses and you pick.
03

The right reader runs

Each kind has its own reader. An inspection gives findings by system; a manual gives upkeep intervals; a policy gives coverages and renewal dates.
04

The facts are filed

Each fact lands on the part of the home it’s about, marked as read from that document. When sources disagree, Eave asks you.

A reader for each kind
of paper that matters.

Recognizing a document is the start. For the kinds that carry facts about the property, a dedicated reader pulls out the parts you’ll need later.

Quoted with the page

Read into the record

Facts land where they belong

A single inspection can touch the roof, the furnace, the electrical panel, and the water heater. Eave reads the findings into each of them, so the roof’s record shows what the inspector said about the roof, and the report is one click away.

Every document row shows what it was read into, with links to each item. Anything Eave filed on its own is marked as read from a document and can be undone.

Needs a decision

When two sources disagree, you choose

The inspection says the roof is 15 to 20 years old. The disclosure says it was replaced in 2019. Eave doesn’t pick one quietly. It leaves the record as it was and shows both, so you can use one, keep both as a range, or dismiss it.

Some sources count for more than others. What you entered comes first, then signed documents, then what someone saw on site, then public data, and Eave’s own guesses come last.

Versions

Documents change.
Eave shows you how.

A revised disclosure, a renewed policy, an updated association budget. When something looks like a newer version of a document you have, Eave asks before it does anything: replace it, keep both, or not related.

01

Every version kept

A replaced document is marked superseded, not deleted. The list shows how many earlier versions there are, and they’re a tap away.

02

What changed, in plain words

Under the current version, a short list of what changed since the last one: the renewal date moved, the deductible went up, a new finding appeared.

03

Who sent it, and when

Each document keeps its sender, from the seller, an inspector, or a lender, and the date on it, so a stack of paper sorts itself into order.

04

Reads in the background

A long report reads while you carry on. Eave lets you know when it’s ready to review.

05

Scans and photos count

PDFs and photos both work, iPhone photos included. A scanned page is read just like a digital one.

06

Yours to remove

Remove a file and it’s deleted for good. What you already confirmed from it stays on your home’s record.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

What kinds of documents can Eave read?

Eave recognizes 46 kinds of property document in seven families, from purchase agreements and inspections to manuals, policies, association papers, and leases. Ten readers pull the facts out of the kinds that carry them. More on documents →

How big can a file be?

You can store files up to 100 MB. Eave reads files up to 15 MB automatically; bigger files are kept on the home all the same. Every plan includes storage: 1 GB on Free and 10 GB on Intelligence.

Is document reading free?

On Residential, setup reads your paperwork on every plan, Free included. Reading documents you add later, from the Documents page, is part of Eave Intelligence at $2.99 a month. Buyers get reads with each home on their plan. See pricing →

Does Eave change my records on its own?

Only when it’s confident, and always with an undo. When a document disagrees with what’s already on the record, it changes nothing and asks you.

What if it reads something wrong?

Fix it or remove it. The mark showing which document it came from stays, so you can always tell what came from paperwork and what came from you.

Who can see my documents?

You and the people you invite to the property. Documents and photos are private to your home and never sold. Data & privacy →
