How the pieces connect

One receipt, all the way through.

No single capability is the point. The point is that each one hands its work to the next, so something you did once keeps paying off. Here’s one ordinary email, followed through the engine.

01Sources

An order confirmation arrives

A new water heater, bought online, lands in the inbox you linked. The next inbox scan finds it and shows it to you: save or skip.

02Document intelligence

The order is read

Eave reads out the store, the date, the price, and the model number, and recognizes that one line is an appliance.

03Home graph

It’s filed where it belongs

The water heater joins the home’s systems with its model and purchase date, matched to the one already on record if there is one.

04Product graph

The model is looked up

Eave pulls what the maker says about that model: how often to flush it, the issues that show up at its age, and the part numbers.

05Plans

The upkeep is suggested

The maker’s flush interval shows up as suggested upkeep. Add it and it’s timed for the season, and the score counts it once it’s done.

06DIY or pro

You pick who does it

Do it yourself with a tutorial picked for the job, or hand it to a contractor from your list, with the cost each time.

What the engine won’t do

Smart about the house.
Careful with you.

The same rules hold in every product, because it’s the same engine.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

Do I have to pay for these features separately?

No. Features aren’t sold on their own. Each Eave product includes the engine, and each product’s plan decides how much of it you get: Residential is free for one home, with Eave Intelligence at $2.99 a month for the full plan. See pricing →

Which features are free?

On Residential, a lot of the engine is free for one home: Sources, typed capture, setup from your paperwork, the home record and house manual, product profiles already in the library, your home’s risk profile, the Upkeep Score, this week’s list, tutorials, and local pro search. Eave Intelligence adds the 12-month plan, voice and photo capture, guided project plans, reading any document, manufacturer lookups, and materials estimates. Free vs. Intelligence →

Is it the same engine in every product?

Yes. A disclosure packet a buyer uploads is read by the same reader that files a homeowner’s closing folder, and the record a seller hands over is the same record a homeowner keeps. That’s why the record can move with the house.

Does Eave decide things for me?

No. Eave reads, files, ranks, and suggests. You confirm what gets saved and decide what gets done. When two sources disagree, Eave shows both and asks.

Where does the information come from?

From you, from the accounts you link, from the documents you hand over, from what manufacturers publish about your models, and from public sources such as FEMA, USGS, and the Census, each shown with its source.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your property’s record is private to you and the people you invite. Connected accounts are read-only, and you can export or delete everything at any time. Data & privacy →
