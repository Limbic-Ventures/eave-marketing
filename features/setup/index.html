Features · Intelligent Home Setup

You already have the paperwork. Let Eave read it.

The closing folder. The utility bills. The manuals in the kitchen drawer and the inspection report you read once. Drop them in and Eave builds your home's record from what's already there.

In practice
"We had a folder three inches thick and I hadn't opened it since the day we signed."

That folder names your title company, your lender, your insurer, the year the roof went on, and the serial number on the water heater. Setup reads it so you don't have to.

How it works

How setup reads your paperwork

1
Give it what you have

Documents, photos of stickers and data plates, a utility bill. Any order, any format, up to 100 MB per file.

2
Eave reads and sorts

It works out what each document is and pulls out what matters: systems and their ages, warranty dates, filter sizes, who your providers are. A long report reads in the background while you carry on.

3
The clear ones file themselves

When Eave is confident, the item is already in place and marked as read from a document. Each one has an undo.

4
You decide the rest

Anything uncertain waits for you, with the page it came from. Eave never decides a judgment call for you, like whether an inspection finding still applies.

Eight short steps, at your pace

Setup walks through your home in eight steps: your home, documents, spaces, structure, systems, utilities, appliances, and your plan. Stop after any of them.

Eave remembers where you were and picks up there. Setup lives under Property settings → Update my home, so you can come back in a month with the manual you found in the garage and carry on.

Turn the inspection report into a plan

Upload the inspection report and Eave lists what the inspector found. Keep the items that still need doing, uncheck the ones the seller fixed, and say why. What's left becomes your first plan, with rough costs, so the water heater warning on page nine doesn't surprise you two winters from now.

Nothing is overdue in your first ninety days.

A house you bought last month doesn't need a list of everything the previous owner skipped. Eave holds off, and your score starts fresh.

Questions

Setup, answered

What should I upload to set up my home?

Whatever you have. The closing folder is the single most useful thing. A utility bill, the appliance manuals, the inspection report, and the hazard disclosure each help on their own, and so does a photo of the data plate on the furnace or water heater. Eave works out what each file is when you drop it in, and if you upload something twice it keeps the copy on file.

How big can a file be?

Up to 100 MB per file, so a full inspection report or a scanned closing packet fits. Every plan includes document storage: 1 GB on Free, 10 GB on Intelligence.

Does Eave decide things for me?

Only when it's confident, only for things you can undo in one tap, and it always shows you what it filed and which page it came from. Which inspection findings still apply is your call, every time.

What if it reads something wrong?

Fix it or remove it. The note about which document it came from stays, so you can always tell what came from paperwork and what came from you.

Is setup free?

Yes. Setup and document reading work on every plan. Voice capture and photo reading of your own snapshots are part of Eave Intelligence.
