Expansion add-on for agents · Escrow Desk

Under contract,
the file becomes a desk.

Eave Partners is free because it’s how you bring clients in. Escrow Desk is the add-on for the deal itself: from the accepted offer to the keys, the work a transaction coordinator does today, kept current and in front of your client instead of in a document that’s out of date by the first extension.

01

A timeline from the contract

Eave reads the executed agreement and every counter, addendum, and extension that follows, counts the days the way the form counts them, and lays out every date and whose it is.

02

Moves when the dates move

An extension lands, Eave proposes the shift, you confirm it in one tap, and the dates that depend on it follow, with the original kept beside the new one.

03

Shared with your client

Your buyer sees the same timeline in their search: days out, what’s due, and what’s theirs to do, with a calendar feed for each person.

04

Versions, and what changed

Every document filed to the transaction. When a new version arrives, see the fields that changed: close of escrow Oct 14 → Oct 21.

05

Send it, and know it was read

Send a document for review and see when it was opened and which pages were seen. Your client can leave a note on the page it’s about.

06

The listing side too

Open the seller’s side at acceptance for what the seller owes the buyer and by when, from the same executed agreement.

Eave counts days; it doesn’t advise. Every date carries the same label in the app, “Computed reference dates, from the dates as written,” with the document it came from. What to do about a date is between you, your client, and their attorney.

Why I built Eave

Nobody hands you a manual
for your home.

For me it started the year we bought our house. We’d done the inspection, waived our contingencies, and moved in knowing there was work ahead. I knocked out the big move-in projects fast, and then my mind went blank. The hundred smaller things the inspection had flagged lived in a notes app, in contractor quotes buried in my inbox, and in the little jolt of I’ve got to get to that every time I passed them on the way out the door.

When I finally sat down to tackle something, I couldn’t tell whether it was over my head or completely doable with a bit of guidance. The AI I asked had no memory of my house. The apps I found all wanted endless data entry. The idea for Eave clicked one night, elbow-deep in a garbage-disposal wiring job that dragged past 2 a.m., the kind of job I could have finished in an hour with the right help instead of a dozen open tabs.

I grew up in a construction family, spent my teenage years on job sites, and then fifteen years building software. Eave sits at that intersection, and it’s the app I need: something that simply remembers, hands me the right specific help, and never overwhelms. And when a house changes hands, all of that shouldn’t walk out the door with the seller.

Questions, answered

Common questions.

I’m already under contract. Can I start there?

Yes. Pick “Made an offer, or under contract” and Eave sets up the one home straight into escrow mode: your dates, the checklist, and every version of every document. If an agent is helping you, invite them from there and they see the same file. Eave for Buyers →

Does the record really move to the buyer?

Yes, when the seller chooses. Systems and their service history, projects and materials, contractors, and documents go with the house. The seller’s login, billing, and other properties stay with the seller. How the hand-off works →

I’m an agent. What does it cost me?

The console is free. You pay only when you cover a client’s Eave with a seat, $2.99 per client per month, or turn on escrow for a home under contract, $10.99 once per home, per side. Never a cut of your commission. Eave Partners →

Is Eave free?

Residential Upkeep is free for one home, forever, with no card. Eave Intelligence is $2.99 a month for the full plan. Eave for Buyers is $3.99 a month or $39.99 once, with a free try on three homes. The Partners console is free. See full pricing →

Do I need to download an app?

No. Eave runs in any browser at app.eavehome.app: phone, tablet, or desktop. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon. Getting started →

What’s the difference between the products?

Two families on one record. Property upkeep keeps a place up: Residential Upkeep for homeowners and Commercial Upkeep for businesses and nonprofits. Property transactions gets you through a sale: Eave for Buyers before you own it, Eave for Sellers to get ready and hand over the record, and Eave Partners for the agents and inspectors who help. Guardian and Pro are add-ons to Residential. Compare on the pricing page →

What about a business, an HOA, or a church?

That’s Commercial Upkeep: the same record and plan for a building, in editions for missions, associations, businesses, multi-site operators, and apartments. Join the waitlist for your edition. Commercial Upkeep →

Is my data private?

Your data is yours. You review what Eave finds before it’s saved, it’s stored with row-level security, and you can export or delete everything at any time. Data & privacy →
