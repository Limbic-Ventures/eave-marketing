Before the listing

Turn your prep list into their to-do list

You walk a house a year out and rattle off a dozen easy wins: regrout the guest bath, service the furnace, repaint the garage door. By listing day, half of them are forgotten. When your seller captures each one in Eave the moment you say it, they show up to photo day ready instead of scrambling.

Eave sorts the list by value and effort for the time before listing, so the work gets done on their schedule, not the week the sign goes up. Fewer surprises at inspection, fewer price chips at the table.

Running a buyer’s search

Every buyer you’re working with,
in one place.

And a record of what you did for them. Start a search for a client before they have an account, line up the candidates, drop the seller’s packet on each one, and see at a glance who needs you today: three homes, one touring, two asks.

Show what you did, in writing

Buyer’s agents now work under a written agreement and need to show what it bought. Every recommendation you made, every document you sourced, every question you answered is on the record, on the house it is about. It is the buyer’s record to keep.

Hand them a home that’s already set up

At close, the house you helped them buy becomes their Eave home, seeded from the search, with your name on it. If the deal falls through, nothing is lost and you are both back on the shortlist.

What you don’t get. Eave never grades neighbourhoods or schools, never turns a photo estimate into an inspection, and never scrapes a listing. You bring the listing material; Eave reads what you give it.

Expansion add-on · Escrow Desk

Under contract, the file becomes a desk.
Kept current, and shared with your client.

Eave Partners is free because it’s how you bring clients in. Escrow Desk is the add-on for the deal itself: from the accepted offer to the keys, the work a transaction coordinator does today, kept current and in front of your client instead of in a document that’s out of date by the first extension. Turn it on for a home when it goes under contract: $10.99 once, per side, the same price your client would pay.

01

Open a transaction

At the accepted offer, one sheet opens the transaction on that home and previews the key dates before you commit.

02

A timeline from the contract

Eave reads the executed agreement and every counter, addendum, and extension that follows, counts the days the way the form counts them, and lays out every date and whose it is.

03

Moves when the dates move

An extension lands, Eave proposes the shift, you confirm it in one tap, and the dates that depend on it follow. Every shift keeps the original date, the new one, the document that moved it, and who confirmed.

04

Shared with your client

Your buyer sees the same timeline in their search: days out, what’s due, and what’s theirs to do. A calendar feed for each person, and email notices they opt into that stop at close.

05

Versions, and what changed

Every document filed to the transaction, packets split into their forms, duplicates recognised. When a new version arrives, see the fields that changed: close of escrow Oct 14 → Oct 21.

06

Send it, and know it was read

Send a document for review and see when it was opened and which pages were seen, in Eave rather than guessed from an email. Your client can leave a note on the page it’s about.

Coming to Escrow Desk: the offers grid and the repair-request view on the listing side, the seller’s own timeline page, forward-to email capture, replies by text, and a close packet with the final timeline and every shift.

For agents, answered

Questions, answered.

What does it cost me?

Setting up Eave Partners is free; it is part of your own Eave account. What you pay for is flat and predictable. A client seat is $2.99 per client per month, bought one at a time or five at a time, and a one-time 12-month gift is also available. A buyer’s search is $3.99 a month for the whole process, hunt through escrow, or $39.99 up front until it closes, with a $19.99 conversion at close on either plan. Eave Leads is a flat subscription. Nothing is a cut of anything. Partner pricing →

Is this a referral-fee arrangement?

No. Eave never pays you for referrals. Eave Leads is homeowner-initiated and priced as a flat subscription.

Does my client have to keep paying after closing?

Their choice. When your funding ends, they keep Intelligence for 30 days, then either take the subscription over or stay on the free plan. The free plan keeps their home’s whole record either way.

What does the client actually get?

The full Eave Residential app: a searchable record of their home’s systems and appliances, an AI upkeep plan, reminders, and their Upkeep Score. It’s the same product a homeowner would sign up for on their own — you’re just the reason they have it.

Can a client disconnect from me?

Yes, any time, from their Membership settings. The seat returns to your pool and you get a note that it happened.

Can I start a search before my client signs up?

Yes. Add the buyer, line up homes, and send one invite. Everything moves to them when they claim it.

Who owns the search?

The buyer, at close. Your fee never buys their private notes.

Does this replace my MLS tools?

No. Eave takes the listing material you already have, a link, a PDF, an address, and does the work the MLS doesn’t. No feed, no scraping.

What is Escrow Desk?

The add-on for the stretch from accepted offer to close. Eave builds the escrow timeline from your executed agreement, moves it when an extension lands, shares it with your client, and keeps every version of every document on the transaction. The console stays free; escrow is turned on for a home when it goes under contract.

What does Escrow Desk cost?

$10.99 once per home, per side, at launch price: the same price your client would pay if they turned it on themselves. It’s charged when you turn escrow on, never at close, and never depends on how the deal turns out. The first one is free for every new firm, and turning the same home back on within 90 days is free.

Does my client pay anything for their escrow?

Only if they turn escrow on themselves. Whoever turns it on for a home pays the $10.99, you or your client, at the same price. A buyer on the $39.99 paid-once search already has it included.

Can the price change on a home I’ve already turned on?

No. An activation is priced when you make it, and a price change never touches one you’ve already made.

Does it replace my transaction coordinator?

It takes over the timeline, the document wrangling, and the “did you read it” chase. Your coordinator still talks to escrow and chases signatures.

Does Eave tell my client what to do about a deadline?

No. Eave counts days and shows where each date came from. What to do about a date is yours to advise.

Can my brokerage roll this out?

Let’s talk.

Is there something for home inspectors?

It’s coming. Your report is the first document a buyer uploads, and we’re building a way for it to keep working after you hand it over. Write to support@eavehome.app and we’ll let you know when it’s ready.
