Support · Importing documents & records

Documents

Upload limits, storage per plan, what Eave reads from a document, privacy, and what delete actually does.

Where they live

Under Memory → Documents: every inspection, manual, warranty registration, declarations page, permit, plan, and quote you've given Eave, with the storage meter at the top.

Uploading

Drag a file in, or add it during setup. Files up to 100 MB upload in one go, straight from your browser to private storage, with a progress bar for the big ones. Long documents are read in the background, so you can keep working.

What Eave reads

Systems and their ages, filter sizes, warranty end dates, renewal dates, and the companies a document names. You confirm what it found before anything is saved to your home. A document can be attached to the thing it's about, so the furnace manual sits with the furnace.

Storage

Every plan includes document storage: 1 GB on Free and 10 GB on Intelligence, counted across every home you own. Photos don't count against it. The meter on the Documents page shows what you've used, and deleting a document frees the space.

Privacy

Your files are yours. Documents and photos are private to your home: only you and the people you've shared your home with can open them; nothing is public. Eave reads a file only when you ask it to, during setup, when you tap Read it, or when you scan something.

Deleting

Deleting a document erases the stored file, not just its entry in the list. It's gone.

Common questions

Quick answers

What happens when I hit the storage cap?

The upload is refused with a message saying how much room is left. Delete something you don't need, or move to Intelligence for 10 GB.

Can I download my originals?

Yes, from the document's row. Your full export includes them too.

Does Guardian change this?

Eave Guardian is coming soon and adds more on top. Today's Documents page is standard storage, included on every plan.
