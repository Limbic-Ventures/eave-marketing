Support · Getting started

Sharing your home with your household

Invite a co-owner, a helper, or a house sitter. Who sees what, how access ends, and why seats are free.

Who you can invite

  • Co-owner. Everything you can do. A home always keeps at least one co-owner, and a co-owner shares the home's plan, so if you have Intelligence, so do they.
  • Helper. Adds, edits, and ticks things off, without the keys to billing or membership.
  • House sitter. Temporary access with an end date. It ends on its own.

How to invite

Open Property settings → People with access, enter an email, pick the role, and send. They accept the invite and see the same home you do.

Temporary access

For a house sitter or anyone else who needs in for a while, pick one week, two weeks, a month, or a date. When it arrives, access ends with no action from you.

Removing someone

You can revoke a helper or a sitter at any time from the same card. A co-owner can remove themselves, as long as the home still has one.

Seats are free

There is no per-person charge on any plan. The house is the unit, not the seat.

Common questions

Quick answers

Does my partner need to pay for Intelligence too?

No. A co-owner shares the home's plan. One Intelligence subscription covers the household.

Can a house sitter see my documents?

They see the home you share for as long as you've given them access. It ends at the date you set.

What about my real-estate agent?

That's a different kind of access. Your agent appears under People → Agents and never holds a household role.
